# Product Brief

## Product Name

Opportunity Radar

## Working Concept

Opportunity Radar helps users discover, compare, and track opportunities relevant to their goals. An opportunity may be a scholarship, internship, grant, job, fellowship, hackathon, competition, startup program, or similar time-bound opening.

## Target Users

- Students looking for scholarships, internships, competitions, and learning programs.
- Early-career professionals tracking jobs, fellowships, grants, or accelerators.
- Community organizers or mentors curating opportunities for a group.
- Administrators who want to publish or verify opportunity listings.

## Core Value

Users should be able to find relevant opportunities faster, understand whether they are eligible, and avoid missing important deadlines.

## Goals

- Centralize opportunity discovery in one searchable place.
- Rank or filter opportunities by relevance, deadline, category, location, eligibility, and status.
- Help users save, track, and act on opportunities.
- Make opportunity quality visible through verification, source links, and freshness indicators.

## Non-Goals For MVP

- Full applicant tracking system.
- Automated application submission.
- Social networking or messaging.
- Payment, subscription, or monetization flows.

## Assumptions

- The first version is web-based.
- Listings can be manually created or imported later.
- Every opportunity has a source URL and a deadline when available.
- Users benefit from reminders and saved lists, but reminder delivery can be basic in the MVP.
